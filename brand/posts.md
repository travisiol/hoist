## 1 — What it is

Launch it. Holders get paid.

HOIST is a launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Every coin launched here gets its own treasury. Its trading fees land there, and the holders who stake share 80% of them, in ETH.

No owner. No admin key. The split is in the contract.
[link]

## 2 — What you gain

Holding a HOIST coin means:

→ 80% of its creator fees go to the holders who stake. Pro rata. Claim any time.
→ The treasury has no key: fees are pulled from the launchpad's escrow by anyone, split 80 / 15 / 5, paid by claim.
→ Unstake when you want. Tokens come back as they went in.

Launching one means your coin pays its holders from day one.
[link]

## 3 — How it works

One coin on HOIST:

1. Launch: name, ticker, creator tax (default 5%). The coin goes live on Pons with its own treasury as fee recipient.
2. Trade: buys and sells pay the creator tax into that treasury.
3. Stake: holders stake the coin and share 80% of every pull. 15% runs the platform, 5% goes to the HOIST vault.
4. Claim: ETH, any time, from the token page.

If nobody is staked when fees arrive, they wait for the first stakers.
